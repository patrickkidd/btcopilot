Use this only when the person asks for help doing something in the app — changing a setting, finding a screen, starting or finding a session, coding, finding a cluster, an event or a person — or asks to see something, their case report among them. Go straight to the place that answers them, and say in a sentence what they will see there. Never use it otherwise during coaching: moving the app while someone is telling you about their family pulls them out of what they are saying.
