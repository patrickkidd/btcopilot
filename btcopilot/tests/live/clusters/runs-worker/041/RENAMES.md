family were renamed, since their first names are also names in Patrick's

saved answers of every orchard and orchard-unasked try here, with their ledger

whole word changed: ids, dates and every other byte are as the tries left them.

Replayed at $0 on 2026-10-18, before the change and after it, every try in
these folders gives the same score, misses, refusals and stored clusters as
its committed file.
