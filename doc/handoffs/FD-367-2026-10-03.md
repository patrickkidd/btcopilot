## Key decisions to preserve (ruled, or binding as project rules)

- **The ten-level order** (Patrick, 2026-10-01; ruling candidate, no id yet because this machine
  had no sops key). The page follows the order the Bowen literature presents a case in, written up
  in the corpus research file MENTAL_MODEL.md: the people on a picture; what brought them, dated;
  the couple since they met; each parent's own family; one calendar; the formulation (the
  reading); the person's own part (your part); where there was a choice; what to work on and
  expect; the effort. Why: the first gallery was period cards with no top level and he could not
  find the hierarchy; the sources give one.
- **Nothing implies more than the record holds** (Patrick, 2026-10-02; binding project rule in
  CLAUDE.md since commit 1dd16d2a). Every reading carries what it rests on and the record's
  coverage; a thin record says "not enough in the record to choose a reading", never a thesis.
  Why: he said it is critical the page never claims to know more than it does, and models break
  that rule by habit.
- **Mockups are the app's own code, pixel for pixel** (Patrick, 2026-10-01). Never a drawn
  stand-in or a cartoon replacement. Why: the earlier galleries drew things the app does not do.
- **Every frame is labelled proposal, view or alternative** (Patrick, 2026-10-02; in CLAUDE.md).
  Why: on version 4 he could not tell whether P1 to P4 were four proposals or four views of one.
- **Prose is the flag for a visual** (Patrick, 2026-10-02; in CLAUDE.md). Prose piling up in a
  feature means that part needs a picture or another simple UI form; a first iteration may still
  carry some reading. Why: the page must explain itself with no wall of text carrying the meaning.
- **His direction on version 4**, kept in version 5: the ten levels as headlines opened one at a
  time while the page still scrolls; one simple coverage picture first so a viewer feels the gaps
  before reading; lean on the app's own drawing of family segments; an executive summary that
  explains the thesis across the sections, qualified by coverage.
- **The "Where there was a choice" box stays.** Patrick, twice: it is key to the theory. It is one
  dated step of the person's own that could have gone another way, with the facts under it and a
  guess to reject; never a plan.
- **Trials, not rulings:** "outcome first" (the reading at the top of the page or at the end) is
  tried both ways; the timeline pinned at the top on the phone with every chip lighting its event

- **The missing books stay thin** (Titelman 1998, Papero 1990 and 2018, Kerr 2019, Frost, the

## What we learned

- **Sub-agents do the work; the coordinator reads briefs and reports only.** The one long session
  spent its own context on deploys, probes and edits; its context became the largest cost. The
  session ends at its flush and the next round starts fresh from the notes on the branch.
- **Three method rules from this ticket's failures** (efficiency skill criteria 66 to 68 in the
  private corpus): never message an agent running inside a Workflow (it forks a second copy that
  edits the same files; one fork rebuilt the page while it was under critique); a Workflow
  script's shared prompt text is frozen once a stage has run (editing it restarts the earlier
  stages; five builder restarts in fifteen minutes); unattended runs never prompt a human (no
  rm -rf, sudo, kill or interactive command in any brief; scratch stays in a gitignored folder and
  is left in place; one permission prompt kills an overnight run).
- **A critic sees every page before Patrick does.** A separate agent, not the designer, checks
  each frame: what it says, whether every mark is explained, whether anything vanished, whether
  the frame says proposal, view or alternative. Version 5 took four critique rounds before its
  gate was clean.
- **Coverage comes from the whole record.** The first case file had one of his three major
  partners and no cousins, aunts or uncles, so the coverage picture was wrong. The version 5 case
  file is regenerated from his whole record (36 people, 84 events, 16 tracked questions) by a
  build script in the corpus; never hand-pick a subset.
- **Dead end: the version 3 shape.** Period cards with no top level, prose-heavy, no formulation
  line, no split between the couple and each parent's family, no play-by-play. He judged it wrong
  because the hierarchy had to be inferred; do not return to it.
- **Dead end: a reply box on the page.** Posting a chat message to the coach from the case page is
  wrong; the page is a claim with evidence, never a workspace. A viewer's question would pin to a
  step or a person instead (a proposal, not ruled).
- **Desktop must add ease and engagement, not just fit.** Proving the layout does not break on a
  desktop was not what he asked; the research file PLAY-BY-PLAY-DESKTOP.md lists what the room
  and the keyboard add (picture and dated strip side by side, a legend always on screen, arrow
  keys stepping the play-by-play), all proposals.
- **Case material never enters this repo.** His record, Client L and Anna live only in the
  corpus's design folder with their PROVENANCE.md; the mockup build reads them from there.
