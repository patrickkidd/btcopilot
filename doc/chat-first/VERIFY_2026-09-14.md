## The two faults found, and their fixes

1. **A person's row in the people list carried a second line** ("no birth on the record ·
   unknown") against the line saying the people list has no second line under a name. Found on
   all three browsers. Fixed on the branch in the shared row renderer; the check now passes.

2. **The scribe wrote a bond's date as a month** — "+ Marcus & Delphine · married · Jan 1970"
   where the spec says "· 1970" — when the coder only said a year. Fixed on the branch in the
   scribe; the check now passes.

## What the walks got wrong, and what was changed in them

- **Two WebKit text comparisons.** WebKit ends a nested title's text with a newline where
  Chromium does not, and two walks anchored their patterns at the end of the words. The shared
  helper now trims trailing whitespace, and both pass in both engines.
- **"Opening the lists changed the page" on a wide window.** With a professional licence the
  drawer is already pinned open, so the button puts the cursor in its search box instead of
  opening anything — the ruled behaviour. The walk now checks that instead.
- **A row held open by a swipe.** It is slid left on purpose to show the actions behind it, so
  its own words sit off the edge while it is open; the edge gate now passes over it and holds
  for everything else.
- **No call had a time limit.** Playwright waits forever by default, so one step that never
  answered ate a whole ten-minute run in silence. The config now gives every call thirty
  seconds.
- **The last screen of the ballot.** Once the ballot is finished, every call that measures or
  screenshots the page stops answering under the test runner, while what the page says can
  still be read. The same steps in the same state answer under plain Playwright, so it is the
  runner and the page together, not something a person meets. That screen is gated by the walk
  that opens it instead.
- **Ratifying takes between ten and twenty-five seconds** — it drafts the guideline rules and
  scores the coach's pass on the way — and the old fixed twenty-second wait failed six checks
  at desktop size while passing at phone size. Under the harness the waits are conditions, and
  it passes at both sizes.

## Two things that make a run unreliable

- **One sandbox, several agents.** A first matrix was corrupted by another agent running its
  own walk against the same database at the same time: votes answered "that item is not on the
  ballot" and every later journey timed out. The results above are from a run with the sandbox
  to itself. The harness has a lock for the goldens; the review walks have none.
- **Fixture order after a ratification.** Running the structure journey immediately after the
  meeting journey found the vote still closed and failed on a dead button. Reset and re-install
  the ballot fixture, and it passes.

## What could not be verified here

  row's ask / explain / in chat, the play-by-play, the events list's grouping and headings, the

  events are reached only through the coding screen.
- **Talking to the coach**, and everything that follows from a live reply: the lines saying what

- **Signing in for the first time**, Face ID, and the add-to-home-screen card, which need a
  fresh device profile.
- **Anything on Patrick's own account** (diagram 6, coding 2), which was not touched.
