The app as it is built today
Every frame below is the real app: its own markup, its own stylesheet, drawn here as a whole screen rather than a picture of one. The people and the moments are the test family — Corinne, Theo and Marcus — never anyone real. Phone frames are 400 across; the wider frames are the same screens in a desktop window.
