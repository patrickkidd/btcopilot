this file. The same moment must read with the same words and the same year in every
frame.

## Coach exchanges, in the app's register

**1 — a year placed**

- Client: `I moved out on my own in 1996`
- Coach: `That's on your line now. What was going on at home that year?`
- edit line written: `+ Corinne · moved out of the family home · 1996`

**2 — a stretch named**

- Client: `walk me through it`
- Coach: `The winter of 1995 is where it turned: after a year of keeping her distance from Theo, Corinne stopped speaking to him altogether.`
- Coach's own question: `What do you remember about the winter it started?`
- the answers it holds out: `[winter 1995]` `[Theo's move]` `[Corinne, age 20]`

**3 — the whole record counted**

- Client: `how much have I told you?`
- Coach: `Sixty moments across five years, in two runs: The year the headaches started and After the diagnosis.`

**4 — a coder's line, in the coding screen**

- Clinician: `And when did your father go down to Arizona, roughly?`
- Client: `March of 1969. He'd just finished his apprenticeship, and he drove the whole way down.`
- coder types: `father moved down to Arizona, March 1969, right after his apprenticeship`
- edit line written: `+ Marcus · moved · Mar 1969`
- when the coder writes only `he moved to Arizona`, nothing is written and one amber line
  asks: `which person is he — Marcus, or the client?`

## Guideline rules the AI drafts out of those settles

1. When a move and a new job fall within a year for the same person, code one moment;
   the move is the moment. *(from 1973 · started at the copper mine · settled 3 to 2)*
2. When coders split between anxiety up and a relationship move on the same moment, both
   hold unless the transcript names one. *(from 1988 · relationship became difficult ·
   settled 4 to 1)*
