# Toward and away moves (W)

For coders: what the sources say about toward and away moves, where to find it, and what is decided. Read it for food for thought while coding. It is not a rule book; section 1 says which rules exist.

- Built 2026-09-26 from the sources listed in [`README.md`](README.md). Every quote below was checked word for word against its source file by the quote checker; the check is repeatable.
- This is the public edition: excerpts from confidential sources are left out, and so are the paths to the source files ([`README.md`](README.md)).
- Ids (W1, W2, ...) are stable; W stands for the toward/away move. Location keys are explained in [`README.md`](README.md).
- Transcripts of recordings are machine transcriptions. A word marked [sic] is as transcribed.
- This page is new. The old SARF literature review had no file for toward and away; it is a candidate coding unit, not one of the eight relationship values in the white paper (status item 7).

## Coder summary

Toward and away is the current candidate for the lowest-inference way to record a relationship move: one mover, one or more recipients, a direction, a date. Nothing about it is ruled. Three questions are open: whether "toward" means more contact or moving to what is wanted (status item 2); whether a move is an event or a standing state between two people (status item 3); and how away differs from distance and cutoff (status item 4). What else should be coded beside it is also open (status item 1).

The passages that matter most:
- W3: Kerr, "People do move toward and away from one another": the phrase is a description in the books, not a defined term.
- W16 and W19: Patrick's two senses. By contact an argument is two toward moves; by desirability it is an away.
- W22: Kerr says conflict gives contact and distance at once, which is why an argument does not fit either sense cleanly.
- W26: Patrick himself says the model is "not totally falsifiable", because a human is the recording instrument.
- W34 and W35: the coders used toward and away as the safe code "when in doubt"; Patrick built the app around the named mechanisms because toward and away is "low resolution".

The main off-theory trap: reading toward as good and away as bad. By Patrick's contact sense a fight is toward and a calm step back is away (W10, W16). The second trap: coding away as emotional distance. A father who lives in another state after a divorce is "just away" (W40).

## 1. Status of the code

### Patrick (writing and rulings)

1. **Is toward and away the default code?** OPEN (OPEN_QUESTIONS 17). Ruling OPEN_QUESTIONS 17 (toward/away is the current default) — see the private edition. The IRR group's written record contradicts itself on the default (status item 10); the reference no longer calls it the default (reading notes relationship-units). The candidates beside it, with the case for and against each, are in that notes file.
2. **Contact or desirability?** OPEN (OPEN_QUESTIONS 14): (a) more or less contact, so an argument is two toward moves (W16, W18); (b) toward what is wanted and away from what is not, so an argument is an away (W19); (c) both, as a direction plus a separate positive or negative tone, as in Patrick's 2020 templates.
3. **A move or a state?** OPEN (OPEN_QUESTIONS 18): (a) a dated move, as the templates and the app record it; (b) a state per pair (W11); (c) positions that moves enter ([`triangles.md`](triangles.md), section 2d).
4. **Away, distance or cutoff?** Left open for the IRR group. Ruling R-0439 (coding judgment calls are left to the IRR group) — see the private edition.
5. **A couple's split, coded as away.** RULED, then marked undecided. Ruling R-0057 (a worked coding example, marked undecided) — see the private edition. R-0511 marks it undecided.
6. **Every coding rule is provisional.** Ruling R-0511 (coding rules are provisional while ground truth is rebuilt) — see the private edition.
7. **Not in the white paper's value space.** “Value space: conflict, distance, reciprocal functioning, child-focus, cutoff, inside (triangle), outside (triangle), defined-self.” Patrick, WP L51. Toward and away are not among the eight values. How the eight break down into toward and away moves appears only in Claude's drafts (CODING section 5).
8. **The fact and the theory are kept apart.** RULED 2026-09-26. Ruling OPEN_QUESTIONS 5 (facts and theory-based readings are kept in separate layers) — see the private edition. A toward or an away is the fact layer; what theory reads into it is not.
9. **How a toward move is drawn.** RULED for the picture only. Ruling R-0115 (how the picture draws a toward move) — see the private edition.

## 2. What the sources wrote

### 2a. Where the words come from

#### Bowen

- <a id="W51"></a>**W51** “the mothers and daughters would get overclose, fight, separate, come back together again, and repeat the cycles over and over” Bowen, FTiCP ch1 L19. The earliest description of alternating toward and away moves in one pair.
- <a id="W1"></a>**W1** “each one constantly moving to gain a little more close comfort or to withdraw from tension, with each move by one requiring a compensatory move by another.” Bowen, FTiCP ch21 L53. A move by one is expected to bring a move by another.
- <a id="W2"></a>**W2** “Always forming a twosome that's together and excluding the other or separating in times of stress.” Bowen, BS1 L139.

#### Kerr

- <a id="W3"></a>**W3** “People are responding to a reality in the relationship process. People do move toward and away from one another.” Kerr, FE3 L92.
- <a id="W4"></a>**W4** “Even though one person's actions may be totally governed by his subjective view of the relationship, those actions constitute an objective reality for the other person.” Kerr, FE3 L92. What a person does is the observable part, whatever drove it.
- <a id="W5"></a>**W5** “Each person is free to move toward or away from the other and to have the other move toward or away from oneself without being threatened.” Kerr, FE3 L100. Said of two very well differentiated people: a move is not by itself a sign of reactivity.
- <a id="W6"></a>**W6** “another relationship phenomenon that has received considerable attention in the biological literature is that of approach and withdrawal, closeness and distance, or attraction and repulsion between organisms.” Kerr, FE2 L189.
- <a id="W7"></a>**W7** “When two organisms are unable to maintain their attachment in sufficient equilibrium to allow both to be comfortable, the more uncomfortable one will distance and attempt to form a new attachment.” Kerr, FE6 L65.

#### Havstad

- <a id="W8"></a>**W8** “Nineteen dieted to goal weight following shifts in active triangles in their nuclear or extended families in which they increased emotional distance from one family member as they increased closeness with another.” Havstad and Sheffield, ART L43.
- <a id="W9"></a>**W9** “Shifts in the relationship system have to do with level of differentiation in the group, balance, which has to do with levels of togetherness and individuality, shifts in that balance, shifts in distance and closeness” Havstad, Havstad-50thSymposium2014 @00:25:25.

### 2b. Patrick's definitions, 2020 to 2025

Each is dated. None is ruled.

#### Patrick (writing and rulings)

- <a id="W10"></a>**W10** “A toward move would indicate that the person sees some value in engaging with that individual, regardless of whether a psychologist would perceive that as positive (BT: "triangling" / "reciprocity") or negative (BT: "conflict"). An away move would indicate the opposite (BT: "distance").” Patrick, CD L20 (2020-09).
- <a id="W11"></a>**W11** “"Toward" / "Away" state for each unique pair of people (Bowen theory lit). ... "Toward": Engagement with other. Inside of a triangle. ... "Away": No engagement. Outside of triangle.” Patrick, CD L30 (2020-10-23). Here toward and away are states of a pair, not moves.
- <a id="W12"></a>**W12** “Toward: More contact/closeness ... Conflict(-), Reciprocity(+), Triangle/Projection(+) (with enough tension) ... Away: Less contact/closeness ... Distance(-)” Patrick, EEM L11 (2021). Conflict is a toward move.
- <a id="W13"></a>**W13** “Toward and aways are "atomic operations" because they are the most basic occurrence in emotional process. They are indivisible and never absent in relationship. They are synonymous with approach and avoid.” Patrick, EEM L19 (2021). "Never absent" is an untested claim.
- <a id="W14"></a>**W14** “The balance between tension and distance is managed through approach and avoid behaviors. An avoid action decreases tension. An approach behavior decreases distance.” Patrick, TDTA L40 (2021).
- <a id="W15"></a>**W15** “toward and away are currently analogous to approach and avoid in biology and animal/human psychology. These definitions are still provisional in Bowen theory.” Patrick, LOG L17 (2021).
- <a id="W16"></a>**W16** “A phone call is a toward move by one person to another. An argument is two simultaneous toward moves from each individual to the other.” Patrick, LOG L19 (2021). The contact sense.
- <a id="W17"></a>**W17** “An amicable resignation from a board position is several simultaneous away moves from the resigner to each of the other board members ... So is avoiding a Christmas holiday. So is spending a few days apart from someone because you had an argument with them.” Patrick, LOG L21 (2021). One act can be away moves to several people at once.
- <a id="W18"></a>**W18** “There's always one mover ... One or more recipients perceive the move (I haven't worked that part out yet fully) ... Toward = increased contact ... Away = decreased contact ... I'm not totally sold on those definitions, but it's a definition to start with.” Patrick, MOD L296.
- <a id="W19"></a>**W19** “A desirability valence (whether something perceived is desirable or undesirable) ... Go towards the desirable; go away from the undesirable.” Patrick, IM L266. The desirability sense.
- <a id="W20"></a>**W20** “"Differentiation": More toward's for a person without compensatory away's or mechanisms in the system at large.” Patrick, CD L36 (2020-10-23). Differentiation defined from the moves it would be used to predict; Patrick calls such loops circular in later notes (REFERENCE).

### 2c. An argument: toward, away, or both?

#### Kerr

- <a id="W22"></a>**W22** “The focus on one another provides emotional contact, and the anger and stubborn refusals to do what the other wants provide emotional distance.” Kerr, FE3 L149. Conflict gives contact and distance at once.
- <a id="W23"></a>**W23** “If person A expresses to person C that he is angry at person B, this communication functions to increase the level of togetherness between A and C. ... In addition, A's anger at B functions to maintain distance between A and B” Kerr, FE6 L49. One remark, a toward and an away to two people.

### 2d. Grain, and a human as the instrument

#### Bowen

- <a id="W52"></a>**W52** “During a very intense period, one family could go through as many as two complete cycles in one day. The time for the average cycle varied between several days and several months.” Bowen, FTiCP ch1 L19. The grain of a cycle: a record at month grain misses most of them. Sample: 3 mother-daughter pairs chosen for the most intense attachments.

#### Patrick (writing and rulings)

- <a id="W24"></a>**W24** “Bowen theory provides definitions that pertain to long-term shifts/moves in a multigenerational history just as much as moment-to-moment shifts/moves in a single conversation. For example, making a phone call is as much a toward move as hanging up the phone is an away.” Patrick, LOG L31 (2021). No rule yet for which grain to code.
- <a id="W25"></a>**W25** “Some examples are; defining which of the many degrees of detail to log, what to call a toward and what to call an away, and what to do with the "triangle" concept when recording observations.” Patrick, LOG L29 (2021).
- <a id="W26"></a>**W26** “The toward and away data model is not totally falsifiable because their definitions are not clear enough to record them without human perception and cognition as the recording instrument.” Patrick, LOG L49 (2021).

### 2e. Toward and away as the parts of a triangle

Patrick's triangle definitions built from toward and away (2020, 2021) are [`triangles.md`](triangles.md) T37, T38 and T40.

#### Patrick (writing and rulings)

- <a id="W27"></a>**W27** “both toward and away moves occur with enough tension, and perhaps only toward moves occur when tension is low enough.” Patrick, LOG L69 (2021). From his own logging of one annual event (example 4c).

### 2f. What a move is not

#### Patrick (writing and rulings)

- <a id="W28"></a>**W28** “Relationship shifts are automatic moves that a person makes in relation to one or more other people. "Automatic" means that it was driven by emotion, without choice” Patrick, WP L53 (2025). A chosen step, such as a defined-self stand, falls outside this definition.

## 5. Readings that look right but are off-theory

| The reading | What the sources say instead | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Toward is good, away is bad. | Toward holds whether the engagement looks positive or negative; a fight is toward in the contact sense. | W10, W12, W16 |
| An argument is moving apart. | By contact it is two toward moves. Kerr: conflict gives contact and distance at once. | W16, W22 |
| Away means emotional distance. | "Away has a little less assumption". Geography, work or divorce can put someone away without any emotional distance being said. | W40, W41, W43 |

| Toward and away is a defined Bowen term. | Kerr uses the phrase as description; Patrick calls the definitions "provisional". | W3, W15 |
| An "avoidant" person, an "approaching" person. | A move is a dated act by a mover toward recipients, not a trait. | W18 |

| A defined-self stand is an away. | Relationship shifts are defined as automatic; a chosen stand is a third kind of act (OPEN_QUESTIONS 13). | W28 |
